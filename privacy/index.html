Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 14, 2026

LifeToLife ("LifeToLife", "we", "us", or "our") operates LifeToLife websites, apps, games, content channels, and internal distribution tools, including integrations with third-party platforms such as Pinterest.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use our services or when our services connect to third-party platforms.

1. Information We May Collect

We may process the following categories of information, depending on the service or integration you use:

  • Information you provide directly, such as messages, feedback, or support requests.
  • Basic technical information generated when you access our websites or services, such as browser type, device type, IP address, access time, and basic server logs.
  • Information provided by a third-party platform after you authorize an integration, such as account identifiers, profile information, boards, Pins, content metadata, publishing status, and analytics that the platform makes available within the permissions you grant.
  • OAuth authorization data, including access and refresh tokens, when needed to operate an authorized integration. We do not request or store your third-party platform password.

2. How We Use Information

We use information only as reasonably necessary to:

  • provide and operate LifeToLife services;
  • authenticate authorized platform connections;
  • create, publish, update, manage, and measure LifeToLife content through authorized platform APIs;
  • maintain service security and prevent abuse;
  • diagnose technical problems and improve reliability;
  • respond to support or privacy requests; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Pinterest Integration

LifeToLife Distribution is an internal publishing workflow intended to manage LifeToLife's own Pinterest Business account and content through the Pinterest API.

When Pinterest authorization is used, LifeToLife requests only the permissions needed for the relevant publishing, board-management, content-reading, or analytics functions. Authorization is handled through Pinterest's supported OAuth process. LifeToLife does not collect Pinterest account passwords or session cookies.

Information received from Pinterest is used only to operate, maintain, and measure the authorized LifeToLife Pinterest integration and is not sold to third parties.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information.

We may disclose limited information to service providers that are necessary to host, secure, automate, or operate our services, or when required by law. Third-party platform data is handled subject to the applicable platform's developer terms and the permissions granted by the account owner.

5. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including operation, security, troubleshooting, legal compliance, and authorized platform integrations.

OAuth tokens and other authentication credentials are stored securely and are removed, revoked, or allowed to expire when they are no longer required. Operational logs may be retained for a limited period for security and reliability purposes.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. Sensitive credentials such as API secrets and OAuth tokens are not intended to be stored in publicly accessible source code or public documentation.

7. Your Choices and Requests

Where applicable, you may revoke a third-party platform connection through that platform's account settings. You may also request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with LifeToLife services, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements.

Privacy requests may be submitted through the LifeToLife website at lifetolife.net.

8. Third-Party Services

LifeToLife services may link to or integrate with third-party services. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. This Privacy Policy does not control the independent practices of third parties.

9. Children's Privacy

LifeToLife does not knowingly use its distribution API integrations to collect personal information from children. If we learn that personal information has been collected in a manner that requires deletion under applicable law, we will take reasonable steps to remove it.

10. International Processing

Because LifeToLife uses internet-based infrastructure and third-party platforms, information may be processed in countries other than the country where a user is located. We take reasonable steps to use service providers and platform integrations in accordance with applicable requirements.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, integrations, or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material revisions are made.

12. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact LifeToLife through lifetolife.net.
